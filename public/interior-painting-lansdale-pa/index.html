RESIDENTIAL INTERIOR PAINTING

The finish is only as good as the preparation underneath it.

A professional interior paint job should do more than change the color of a room. It should make the entire space feel cleaner, sharper and more finished.

At Cascade Painting, we focus on the details that affect the final result — surface preparation, repairs, clean lines, consistent coverage and respect for the home throughout the project.

We provide interior painting services throughout Lansdale and surrounding Montgomery County communities.

THE CASCADE APPROACH

Careful work from prep to walkthrough.

Before the first coat goes on, we take time to prepare the room properly. Floors and surrounding surfaces are protected, problem areas are addressed and the project is organized before painting begins.

Throughout the work, we focus on clean lines, consistent coverage and keeping the space orderly. When the project is complete, we walk through the finished work with you.
