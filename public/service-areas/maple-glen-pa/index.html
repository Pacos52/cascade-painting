What Cascade brings to a Maple Glen project.

Our Maple Glen portfolio includes finished interior work that reflects the part of painting we emphasize most: what happens before the finish coat. Wallpaper removal, wall preparation, repair and clean trim transitions can determine whether a room reads as truly finished.

When wallpaper or damaged surfaces are involved, the condition underneath is evaluated after removal. Cleaning, repair, sanding and priming are handled as needed before the new finish is applied. That process is defined in the scope rather than treated as an afterthought.
