North Wales, PA · Interior painting

A brighter North Wales living room built around light, not louder color.

This room already had strong architecture, skylights and dark flooring. The goal was to quiet the wall color so the light, fireplace and existing finishes could become the focus.

The project

A finish that works with the space already there.

The room had several strong visual elements competing with one another. Rather than adding another statement color, the project moved toward a restrained neutral finish that let the vaulted ceiling, skylights and whitewashed fireplace carry the space. The success of a change like this depends on keeping edges, ceiling transitions and repaired areas clean because lighter finishes make inconsistencies easier to notice.
