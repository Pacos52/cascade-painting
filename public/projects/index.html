How project pages help

Before-and-after photos become more useful when the project has a story.

A good project page will show more than a finished room.

As new projects are documented, each useful case study can include the service type, town, initial condition, preparation, color or finish decisions, key challenges and final photography. That creates a better resource for homeowners while also giving search engines genuinely unique local content rather than duplicated town pages.

What to photograph on future projects

  • A wide “before” photo from an angle you can repeat after completion.
  • Close-ups of problem areas and preparation work.
  • One or two in-progress photos that show protection and workmanship.
  • The final room or exterior from the same before angle.
  • Several clean finished details for service pages and social media.

When a project has enough material, we can link its case study back to the matching service page and, where appropriate, to a genuinely useful local-area page.
