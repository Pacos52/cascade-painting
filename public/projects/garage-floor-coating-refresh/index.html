Montgomery County · Garage & floor coating

Turning a garage into a space that feels finished instead of forgotten.

Garages are utility spaces, but they do not have to look unfinished. This project paired a cleaner wall system with a coated concrete floor so the entire space read as one finished environment.

The project

A finish that works with the space already there.

Coating concrete starts with evaluating what is already on the slab and what may interfere with adhesion. Walls, base details and floor preparation have to be sequenced so one part of the project does not compromise another. The result is less about making a garage decorative and more about making it cleaner, brighter and easier to maintain.
