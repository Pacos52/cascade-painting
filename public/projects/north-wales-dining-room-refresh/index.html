North Wales, PA · Interior painting

A dining room refresh that kept the traditional details and simplified the palette.

The room already had detailed trim and warm hardwood. The painting work was about making those permanent features read more clearly instead of competing with them.

The project

A finish that works with the space already there.

Traditional rooms can become visually busy when wall color, trim profiles, flooring and furnishings all ask for equal attention. A quieter wall finish creates separation and lets the millwork do its job. The technical part is making sure the chair rail, crown, corners and repaired surfaces stay crisp under strong room lighting.
