About Cascade Painting

A local painting company built around accountability.

Veteran and family owned, based in Lansdale, and focused on making the contractor experience clearer from estimate through final walkthrough.

Cascade Painting is a veteran and family-owned painting company based in Lansdale, Pennsylvania.

The company is built around a straightforward idea: the customer should be able to expect professional workmanship without the uncertainty that often comes with hiring a contractor. That means a clear scope, respectful communication, careful preparation and a finished project that is reviewed rather than rushed out the door.

Local ownership changes the experience.

When a company is small and locally operated, the work and the reputation stay close together. Cascade Painting serves homeowners and businesses in and around Montgomery County, and the goal is to build repeat work and referrals by being the kind of contractor people are comfortable recommending.

Veteran and family owned

Those are not just labels on the website. They shape the way the business is presented: accountability, direct communication, organized work and respect for the property. We want the project to feel considered from the first conversation through the final walkthrough.

Based in Lansdale

Our business address is 24 Green St, Lansdale, PA 19446. The current primary service area includes Lansdale, Ambler, Blue Bell, Chalfont, Plymouth Meeting and Willow Grove, with nearby projects considered based on scope and scheduling.
