Why Cascade exists

Craftsmanship and a professional experience should come together.

Cascade Painting is veteran and family owned and based in Lansdale. The company was built around a simple idea: the finish matters, but so does the way a homeowner experiences the project.

That means clear scope before work starts, protection of the space, repairs handled before finish coats, straightforward communication while the work is underway and a deliberate walkthrough at the end. Staying owner-led and locally focused keeps accountability close to the project instead of buried behind layers of handoffs.

We serve homeowners and select businesses throughout Lansdale and nearby Montgomery County communities, including North Wales, Ambler, Blue Bell and Maple Glen.
