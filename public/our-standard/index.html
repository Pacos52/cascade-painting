The Cascade Standard

The finish matters. So does everything that happens before it.

A professional painting project should feel organized, respectful and predictable from the first conversation through the final walkthrough.

Real Cascade project
01

Understand the room.

We start with the surfaces, the condition, the goals and the way the space needs to function while work is underway.

02

Define the scope.

Painting, repairs, prep, protection and exclusions are discussed before the project is treated like a schedule.

03

Protect and prepare.

Floors, furniture and adjacent surfaces are protected. Repairs and prep are completed before finish work begins.

04

Communicate while we work.

You should know what is happening, what comes next and who to contact without chasing the contractor for answers.

05

Finish with discipline.

Coverage, lines, edges and touch points are checked as the room comes together.

06

Walk it together.

The project closes with a walkthrough and attention to the final details, not a van disappearing as soon as the last coat dries.
